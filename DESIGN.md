# `<i>schedule` Design System

## Direction

Operate-first schedule utility for KMITL students. The interface prioritizes scanability, touch-friendly controls, and a calm data surface over decorative effects.

## Tokens

- Light ground: cool near-white with a lively orange action accent.
- Dark ground: blue-gray surfaces with a bright but softened orange accent.
- Borders are quiet 1px separators; elevation uses restrained soft shadows.
- Rounded corners are reserved for panels and controls; pills are used for compact navigation states.
- IBM Plex Sans Thai handles UI copy and IBM Plex Mono handles times, codes, and measurements.

## Interaction

- The navbar compresses into a floating pill after scroll.
- Theme switching is persistent and respects the system preference on first visit.
- Mobile and tablet expose a card summary before the full horizontally scrollable timeline.
- Motion is restrained and disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

## Accessibility

Use semantic headings, labels, native controls, visible focus rings, readable contrast, and text alternatives for every icon-only control.
